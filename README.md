## 📌 Project Overview

**SmartMart Sales Analysis** is a retail data analytics project focused on analysing supermarket transaction data to identify meaningful sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and branch-level performance.

The project transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making and improve retail business performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and gross income.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Evaluate branch-wise sales performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Analyse payment method preferences.
- Examine customer ratings and satisfaction.
- Generate meaningful business insights and recommendations.

## 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### 1. Product Line Analysis
- Food and Beverages recorded the highest sales.
- Sports and Travel achieved the second-highest sales.
- Health and Beauty recorded the lowest sales.

### 2. Branch Performance
- Giza (Naypyitaw) achieved the highest sales.
- Alex (Yangon) ranked second.
- Cairo (Mandalay) ranked third.

### 3. Customer Analysis
- Member Customers: 565 transactions.
- Normal Customers: 435 transactions.
- Member customers contributed the majority of transactions.

### 4. Payment Method Analysis
- E-wallet: 345 transactions.
- Cash: 344 transactions.
- Credit Card: 311 transactions.

E-wallet was the most frequently used payment method.
